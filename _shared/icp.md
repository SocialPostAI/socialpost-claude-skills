# Who these skills are for

Primary user: a founder or CEO of a small B2B company with no marketing hire,
whose buyers are on LinkedIn. They have 30–60 minutes a week for social, not
hours. They want to look like they have a marketing team.

Implications for every skill in this pack:
- LinkedIn is the primary platform. Other platforms are adaptations, never
  the default.
- Assume business context (pipeline, buyers, positioning), not creator/
  influencer context (follower counts, virality, monetization).
- Respect their time: ask the minimum questions needed, produce finished
  deliverables, and default to sensible choices they can override.
- Success metrics are business metrics: conversations started, profile-to-site
  clicks, inbound leads — never raw follower counts or impressions alone.
- Write outputs a busy founder can act on same-day.
