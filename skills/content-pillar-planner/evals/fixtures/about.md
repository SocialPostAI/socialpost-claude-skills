# About: Dana Whitfield

## Who they are
Dana Whitfield, founder and CEO of Meridian Test Systems. Spent 12 years as a
validation engineer in medical device plants before founding the company.

## Company
Meridian Test Systems builds automated QA and validation rigs for medical
device manufacturers. 11 people, founder-led sales.

## Who they sell to (ICP)
Quality and validation engineers, and VPs of Quality, at 50-500 person
medical device companies. The buying situation: validation is still run on
paper protocols, audits eat weeks, and the quality team is the bottleneck for
every product change.

## The offer
Hardware plus software validation rigs. Sold demo first, then a paid pilot on
one production line, then an annual contract.

## Credibility (3 points)
- 12 years running validation protocols by hand before building the product.
- Rigs installed in 30+ medtech plants.
- Founder still runs the first pilot install personally.

## Opinions they'll defend publicly (3)
1. Paper-based validation is a patient-safety risk, not just an efficiency
   problem.
2. Most QA automation vendors have never run a validation protocol
   themselves, and their products show it.
3. FDA auditors trust automated evidence trails more than binders, whatever
   the industry folklore says.
